The public website of the Agricultural Robotics and Technology Systems (ARTS) Laboratory, under the Agribiosystems Machinery and Power Engineering Division (AMPED), Institute of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering (IABE), College of Engineering and Agro-industrial Technology (CEAT), University of the Philippines Los Baños (UPLB).

The site presents the laboratory's work in agricultural robotics, mechatronics, IoT, data analytics, machine vision, and artificial intelligence, along with its facilities, research outputs, and training offerings.

## Pages

- `index.html` — laboratory overview, focus areas, and mentorship
- `about.html` — laboratory identity and institutional context
- `research.html` — research themes and approach
- `publications.html` — selected research outputs
- `facilities.html` — laboratory equipment, platforms, and infrastructure
- `training.html` — workshops and training-booking information
- `contact.html` — laboratory contact details and embedded Google Map

## Development

This is a dependency-free static website. Open `index.html` in a browser for a local preview, or serve the repository directory with any static web server.

Styles are in `assets/css/style.css`, and small navigation interactions are in `assets/js/main.js`. Laboratory illustrations and locally hosted Lucide icons are stored in `assets/images/`; third-party notices are listed in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`.

## Publishing

The repository is intended for GitHub Pages. Changes pushed to the publishing branch are served at the repository's configured GitHub Pages address.
