Training & Workshops

Applied learning in AI, data, and agricultural technology.

ARTS workshops translate laboratory experience into practical learning for agricultural and biosystems engineering contexts.

Current Offerings

Workshops grounded in applied agricultural technology.

Sessions combine technical foundations with hands-on activities that connect tools, data, and real engineering workflows.

Workshop

Deploying Local Large Language Models

A practical workshop on using locally deployed LLMs for private, controllable, and offline-capable AI applications. Participants explore local model setup with LM Studio, document analysis and retrieval-augmented generation, code generation, AI agents, and data-analysis workflows.

Local LLMsLM StudioRAGAI agentsData analysis
Workshop

Practical Dashboarding with Looker Studio

A hands-on workshop on building dashboards for agricultural planning and decision-making. Participants prepare and clean datasets, connect Google Sheets data sources, create KPIs, charts, maps, and filters, then use the results to identify gaps and prioritize actions.

Looker StudioData cleaningData visualizationKPIsPlanning
Workshop

IoT Deployment through ThingsBoard

A hands-on workshop on connecting sensors and devices to ThingsBoard for telemetry collection, device management, dashboard creation, and agricultural monitoring. Participants learn how to move data from field devices to a self-hosted platform for visualization and decision support.

ThingsBoardIoTTelemetryDevice managementDashboards
Workshop

CNN Deployment for Object Detection in Agricultural Systems

Grounded in ARTS work on plant and weed detection, CNN-based precision spraying, and field machine vision, this workshop guides participants in deploying object-detection models for agricultural tasks. Participants prepare image data, configure camera inputs, run local or edge inference, and evaluate detection performance for field-facing applications.

CNNObject detectionMachine visionEdge AIPrecision agriculture
