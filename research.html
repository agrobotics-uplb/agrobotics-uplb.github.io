Research Areas

Robotics, sensing, and intelligent systems for agriculture.

ARTS focuses on internationally recognized themes in agricultural robotics, mechatronics, smart agriculture, precision agriculture, machine vision, IoT, UGVs, UAVs, localization, navigation, and AI in agriculture.

Smart Farming Framework

AI is useful when it improves a real agricultural decision.

Recent ARTS AI work starts from field constraints: scarce data, expensive compute hardware, local operating conditions, and the need for systems that can be validated safely outside the laboratory.

Decision-First Modeling

Projects begin by defining the farm decision, then matching it to available data and appropriate classification, regression, or advisory workflows.

Problem formulationData quality
