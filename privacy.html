Privacy

A small, privacy-conscious static site.

ARTS Lab publishes research and contact information without collecting visitor submissions or building visitor profiles.

What this site collects

This site has no forms, accounts, cookies, advertising pixels, analytics scripts, or embedded maps. The site JavaScript only controls navigation, page highlighting, the current year, and scroll presentation in your browser.

GitHub Pages may process technical request information as part of hosting the site. That processing is governed by GitHub's policies, which are separate from this site.

Information published here

The site contains professional names, institutional roles, public research outputs, institutional affiliations, a generic laboratory email address, and a public laboratory location. It does not intentionally publish personal phone numbers, home addresses, private email addresses, or other non-public personal information.

External services

Links to Google Maps, Google Scholar, publication repositories, and the IABE website leave this site. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. This site does not embed those services or send them visitor data.

Corrections and removal requests

For corrections to published information or privacy concerns, contact the lab at robotics.uplb@up.edu.ph. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information by email unless it is necessary to resolve the request.
