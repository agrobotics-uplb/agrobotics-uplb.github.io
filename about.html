About The Laboratory

Mentoring research and innovation in agricultural technology systems.

The Agricultural Robotics and Technology Systems Laboratory supports guided research, prototype development, data analytics, and AI-enabled innovation for smart and sustainable agriculture.

Lab Profile

ARTS is based in the Agribiosystems Machinery and Power Engineering Division (AMPED) of the Institute of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering (IABE), College of Engineering and Agro-industrial Technology (CEAT), University of the Philippines Los Baños. The laboratory supports students, faculty, researchers, and technical personnel working on engineering solutions for agricultural systems.

  • Robotic arms, unmanned ground vehicles, unmanned aerial vehicles, and mechatronic implements.
  • Localization, navigation, machine vision, and AI-based crop monitoring systems.
  • IoT telemetry dashboards, machine learning, and LLM-assisted advisory tools.
Principles

How we work

ARTS connects supervised learning, hands-on prototyping, applied research, and knowledge-sharing activities around agricultural technology challenges.

Mentored Research

Students and collaborators receive guidance from concept development through research output preparation.

Prototype Culture

Activities emphasize functional prototypes, datasets, software tools, and technical documentation.

Public Value

The laboratory contributes to work on food security, sustainability, innovation, and climate-resilient farming systems.
