About The Laboratory

Mentoring research and innovation in agricultural robotics.

The Agricultural Robotics and Technology Systems program supports guided research, prototype development, and innovation in smart and sustainable agriculture.

Lab Profile

ARTS is hosted by the Institute of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering, College of Engineering and Agro-industrial Technology, University of the Philippines Los Banos. The program mentors students, faculty, researchers, and technical personnel in engineering solutions for agricultural systems.

  • Robotic arms, unmanned ground vehicles, unmanned aerial vehicles, and mechatronic implements.
  • Localization, navigation, machine vision, and AI-based crop monitoring systems.
  • IoT telemetry dashboards, machine learning, and LLM-assisted advisory tools.
Principles

How we work

ARTS is designed as a capacity-building ecosystem that links mentorship, hands-on prototyping, applied research outputs, and knowledge-sharing activities.

Mentored Research

Students and collaborators receive guidance from concept development through research output preparation.

Prototype Culture

Activities emphasize functional prototypes, datasets, software tools, and technical documentation.

Public Value

The program targets food security, sustainability, innovation, and climate-resilient farming systems.
