Agricultural Robotics and Technology Systems Laboratory

Smart farming through robotics, data, and AI.

ARTS develops agricultural robotics, mechatronics, data analytics, machine vision, IoT, and AI-assisted decision-support systems for smart, sustainable, and climate-resilient farming.

What We Build

Applied robotics, mechatronics, data analytics, and AI for smart farm operations.

Based at AMPED, IABE, CEAT, UPLB, ARTS brings together students, faculty, researchers, and technical personnel to develop and evaluate engineering solutions for agriculture.

Field Robotics

Robotic arms, unmanned ground vehicles, unmanned aerial vehicles, and mechatronic implements for farm operations.

Agri-Sensing

AI-based machine vision, crop monitoring, localization, navigation, and data workflows for agricultural diagnostics.

Smart Systems

IoT monitoring, telemetry dashboards, machine learning, and LLM-assisted advisory tools for decision support.

Mentorship and Learning

Guided learning for applied agricultural technology.

ARTS supports students and collaborators working on agricultural technology research through consultations, hands-on learning, and supervised development activities. Opportunities are aligned with active laboratory work, available facilities, and partner needs.

  • Research guidance for problem formulation, methods, experimentation, and communicating results.
  • Hands-on learning in prototyping, sensing, data analysis, and AI through active laboratory activities.
  • Collaborative support from initial concepts through testing, documentation, and demonstrations when appropriate.
AI For Smart Farming

From sensors to decisions.

ARTS approaches AI in agriculture by asking what decision needs improvement, whether reliable local data exists, and whether AI adds measurable value under field conditions.

Representative Data

Models are built around locally relevant crop, sensor, weather, machinery, and field data rather than generic assumptions.

Field-Ready Computing

Low-cost modular edge computing lowers power demand and supports reconfigurable AI systems for mobile platforms.

Decision Support

Dashboards, machine learning, and AI-assisted tools translate raw readings into practical farm and greenhouse decisions.
